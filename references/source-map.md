## Precedence

When sources disagree, prefer:

1. The user's instruction and `DESIGN.md`.
2. The Floor in `craft-floor.md`.
3. Current official platform and accessibility guidance.
4. The project's existing, deliberate conventions.
5. The heuristics in this skill.
